Morsel

A groovebox for the Novation Launchkey Mini MK4 25. Morsel takes over its pads, encoders, buttons and screen and makes it a sampler, step sequencer and song arranger.

In your browser

Morsel

  • Drum and keys tracks, step sequencing and live recording, chopping samples onto pads, an effects rack per track and songs from patterns.
  • Plug in the Launchkey and open Morsel in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Without one you can still look around.
  • Nothing to install. Your samples and projects stay in your browser.

For Ableton Live 12

Morsel for Live

A control surface script that gives Live the same Launchkey: Morsel's pad modes and encoder pages, step sequencing, clip launching, Live's browser and chopping a sample onto a Drum Rack, all on the Launchkey's screen. Works with Live 12 Lite, which comes with the Launchkey.

Download for Live ↓
How to install
  1. Unzip it and put the Morsel folder into Remote Scripts in Live's User Library (make the folder if it isn't there): on a Mac ~/Music/Ableton/User Library/Remote Scripts, on Windows Documents\Ableton\User Library\Remote Scripts.
  2. Restart Live.
  3. In Settings → Link, Tempo & MIDI, remove the Launchkey Mini MK4 control surface and add Morsel, with Input Launchkey Mini MK4 25 (DAW Out) and Output Launchkey Mini MK4 25 (DAW In).
  4. Under the ports, set the DAW Out input to Track and Remote on, and keep Track on for MIDI Out.
