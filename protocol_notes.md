# Launchkey Mini MK4 25: DAW-mode facts measured with Morsel (2026-10-17)

| What | Measured | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Web MIDI port names | In: `Launchkey Mini MK4 25 MIDI Out`, `… DAW Out`. Out: `… MIDI In`, `… DAW In` | Matched on the substrings `Launchkey` and `DAW` / `MIDI`. |
| Identity reply | `F0 7E 00 06 02 00 20 29 41 01 00 01 01 03 …` (17 bytes) | Sent in reply to `F0 7E 7F 06 01 F7`. |
| Bitmap ack | `F0 00 20 29 02 13 09 F7` | The guide prints `09 7F F7`. That form is wrong, and `isBitmapAck` accepts both. The firmware holds exactly the 8-byte form and sends it once the frame is drawn ([firmware](firmware_notes.md#sysex-commands)). |
| Encoders | Relative: CC 85–92 (`BF 55…5C`) on ch16, `41` = +1 per detent | Measured after the app sent `B6 45 7F` (relative mode on). No absolute CC 21–28 messages arrived. |
| Encoder popups | The device shows its own popup (numeric "0") on every turn unless each encoder target 0x15–0x1C gets config `01` | Config `00` does **not** stop the popup; it only hides one that's showing. An arrangement with bits 5 and 6 clear does. The firmware: bit 6 = show when the control moves on the device, bit 5 = show when the DAW sends a value; both on by default ([firmware](firmware_notes.md#display)). |
| Encoder page arrows | Up = CC 51 (`B0 33`), down = CC 52 (`B0 34`), value 7F/00, on **ch1** | The guide says ch16. The app now accepts button CCs on ch1 and ch16. |
| Play / Record | Play = CC 115 (`B0 73`), Record = CC 117 (`B0 75`), on ch1 | So CC 117 is Record, not Shift. |
| Pad up / down | Up = CC 106 (`B0 6A`), down = CC 107 (`B0 6B`), on ch1 | LEDs on ch4 work (confirmed by eye). |
| Shift | CC 63 on **ch7** (`B6 3F 7F` / `B6 3F 00`) | Measured 2026-10-17. |
| Shift overlay | Holding Shift makes the device draw its own "Plugin \| Drum" (encoder mode \| pad mode) over the screen | Under Live, the DAW label off message (`04 22 01`) doesn't stop it (2026-10-28), so the Live script covers it the same way. Our own temporary text covers it (measured 2026-10-18). Morsel shows two lines of shift shortcuts (`Launchkey.shiftCover`, per mode) on Shift down, and on release cancels them (config 0 on the temporary target) and redraws the bitmap. The bitmap itself shows a full shift map while Shift is held; whether it is visible under the text popup is not measured yet. `Launchkey.SHIFT_COVER = "cancel"` skips the text and relies on the bitmap. 2026-10-20: with the text cover the device still showed "Plugin \| DAW", so it seems to draw its overlay after our reply. "cancel" (close the popup) doesn't hide it either. **What works (measured 2026-10-20): send the shift-map bitmap to the temporary target 0x21** (`bitmapSysex(DISPLAY.temporary, …)`), at 0/40/120/250/500 ms after Shift goes down and then every 700 ms while held; on release, config 0 on 0x21 and redraw the stationary bitmap. Default `shiftCoverMode = "bitmap"`; Lab → Shift popup switches strategies for testing. Shift arrives as `B6 3F 7F` / `B6 3F 00` on the DAW port. |
| Shift + Pad up | CC 102 (`B0 66 7F` / `00`) on ch1, **instead of** CC 106 | The device's own "< Track" function. Morsel uses it for the previous track. Shift + Pad down (presumably "Track >", CC 103) not measured yet. Morsel assumes CC 103 and uses it for the next track; Pad down with Shift held is also accepted. Browse and Chop moved to Shift + the encoder page arrows. |
| Shift + encoder | Nothing arrives: turning an encoder while Shift is held sends no MIDI on either port | Measured 2026-10-24 in the Lab (only `B6 3F 7F` / `B6 3F 00`). So Shift + encoder can't be used, and the fine steps Morsel applies with Shift only reach the on-screen encoders. The firmware passes each turn on with a delta of 0 while Shift is held. A host `B6 3F 00` writes the same Shift state, untested ([firmware](firmware_notes.md#shift)). |
| Button LEDs | `B3 <cc> <brightness>` (CC on ch4) lights the encoder page arrows (CC 51/52), Play and Record | Confirmed by eye. The buttons *send* on ch1, but their LEDs listen on ch4. |
| Bitmap rate | ~12 frames/s, paced by the ack | Each 1216-byte frame is sent only after the previous one is acked. |
| Pad layout after connect | The device reports `daw` (CC 29 on ch7) right after the handshake, even though the handshake asked for `drum` | The controller now sends the layout the current mode needs again (at most once a second). Confirmed 2026-10-17: Play mode plays with velocity straight after connecting. After a page reload the report arrives within the first second; the controller used to skip it then (its rate limit started at page load), leaving the pad LEDs on the wrong layout's notes and the pads dark. The pad LEDs are also resent 300 ms after the handshake. |
| Device pad modes (chords) | Shift + pad picks one of the device's own pad modes. Measured 2026-10-25: Shift down reports layout `daw` (`B6 1D 02`, the pad-mode picker), picking a chord mode reports `B6 1D 0F` (15), then Shift up. Morsel answered the `daw` report by sending its drum layout, which landed after the pick and knocked the chord mode out (the pads went on sending drum notes on ch10). | Layout reports are ignored while Shift is held; Shift up without a pick puts Morsel's layout back. Any CC 29 value other than 1 / 2 is the device's own mode (`nativePads`): Morsel sends no pad LEDs and doesn't fix the layout. Func / a mode change / a drum track take the pads back (`setLayout`). Not measured yet: where the chord notes arrive (expected: the keys port), whether the encoders and screen go to the chord mode. |
| Under Live (Morsel control surface, 2026-10-25) | Scene = CC 104 (`B0 68`), Func = CC 105 (`B0 69`), both on **ch1**, 7F / 00. Asking for the drum layout (`B6 1D 01`) is answered with `B6 1D 02` and then `B6 1D 0F` (15), and the pads then play drum notes into a Drum Rack. Asking for `daw` (2) is answered with 2. The 1225-byte bitmap goes through Live's `send_midi` and is acked as above; the screen shows it. `c_instance.note_repeat` repeats the drum-layout pads (not forwarded to the script, Track on). | So 15 is not only the device's chord modes: right after Morsel asks for drum, 15 means drum. The firmware: 15 is the drum layout on the DAW port. In DAW mode CC 84 (set to 127 by DAW mode on) turns a request for 1 into 15 ([firmware](firmware_notes.md#layouts-cc-29--30--31)). Encoders, Play, the page and pad arrows and Shift arrive exactly as measured with Web MIDI. |
| Pad RGB under Live (2026-10-28) | `F0 00 20 29 02 13 01 43 <note> r g b F7` on the drum-layout pads (notes 36–51) lights nothing; palette notes on the same pads do. Live's Launchkey_MK4 script sends only palette values (`liveobj_color_to_value_from_palette`). | The firmware explains it: `01 43` writes the DAW-layout pads' LED table; the drum-layout pads are `01 63 <note> r g b` ([firmware](firmware_notes.md#leds)). Both measured 2026-10-31: `01 43 70 7F 00 00` turns the bottom-left DAW-layout pad red, and `01 63` lights the drum layout. The Live script sends static Live colours this way on both layouts; pulsing and flashing pads keep the nearest palette colour. |
| Encoder touch under Live (2026-10-28) | After `B6 47 7F` (touch output on), an encoder sends a CC on **ch15** with its number (1–8 = CC 85–92): `BE 55 7F`, then `BE 55 00` about 80 ms later. It comes only while the encoder is **turned**; a finger resting on the knob sends nothing. | The Mini MK4 has no real touch, so touch-to-preview can't work. The Live script doesn't use it. |
| Live connection under Live (2026-10-28) | `F0 00 20 29 02 13 02 7F F7` (Live's Launchkey_MK4 hello) is echoed back. The firmware: it switches DAW mode on like `9F 0C 7F` and replies with the state now in force ([firmware](firmware_notes.md#daw-mode-and-the-daw-states)). After it, Shift + the Chord Map pad reports CC 29 = **14** while Shift is held, while the drum layout reports 15. | The Live script sends it after DAW mode on, and on Shift up hands the pads to the device's mode when a pick (not 1 / 2 / 15) was reported during the hold. |
| Scale CCs under Live (2026-10-29) | Root: CC 61 / 81 on ch7, 0–11. Scale: CC 62 / 80, the position in the firmware's 30 scales **counted from 1** (Major = 1, Minor = 2, Dorian = 3; 0 = no scale), as Live's Launchkey_MK4 builds `SCALE_NAMES = [None] + …`. At connect the device reports `B6 3D 00`, `B6 3E 01` (C Major). | Both directions work in the Live script. |
| Encoders in Live's MIDI map (2026-10-29, from Live's scripts) | `_Framework.ControlSurface._install_mapping` calls `Live.MidiMap.map_midi_cc_with_feedback_map(midi_map_handle, parameter, channel, cc, map_mode, feedback_rule, avoid_takeover, mapping_sensitivity)` with a `CCFeedbackRule` (`channel`, `cc_no`, `cc_value_map`, `delay_in_ms`). Launchkey_MK4 maps CC 85–92 on ch16 with `relative_smooth_binary_offset`. Sensitivity (`ableton/v3/control_surface/parameter_mapping_sensitivities`): continuous 1.0; quantized 0.1 × (1 if on/off else range / 8). | The Live script maps the encoders on Live parameters in Play / Track / Clips this way; confirmed on the hardware 2026-10-29 (smooth, lists step well, one undo step per turn). |
| Compressor sidechain from a script (2026-10-29) | `CompressorDevice.available_input_routing_types` lists the tracks (e.g. Drums, Keys, A-Reverb, B-Delay, Main, No Input); setting `input_routing_type` works in Live 12 Lite. Right after it, `available_input_routing_channels` is `['']`; Live lists the source's channels a moment later. For a Drum Rack it lists six per pad: `Kit \| Pad \| Pre FX / Post FX / Post Mixer` and the same a level deeper with the sample's name, plus the track's own Pre FX / Post FX / Post Mixer. | The Live script's DUCK page picks the channel a tick later, offers one entry per pad (its Post FX, named by the pad) plus the whole track, and finds the kick by name (kick, bassdrum, bass drum, bd). |
| Keybed under Live (2026-10-28) | After `B6 4C 7F` (keyboard to DAW), the keys arrive on the DAW port on **ch9** (`98 3C 17` / `88 3C 00`). | The Live script maps them to the pads on drum tracks. |
| Commands found in the firmware (Lab, 2026-10-31) | All work: `01 63 <note> r g b` lights the drum-layout pads in RGB, and one `01` message can carry several entries. `06 23 00 <text>` puts that text in the device's Shift overlay in place of "Drum". `04 23 01` stops the device's "Pad Mode" popup. `B6 3F 00` sent while Shift is held makes the encoders send CC 85–92 again, and the device's Shift still works afterwards. But the device echoes it at once as `B6 3F 00`, and letting go of Shift then sends nothing (the state is already off), so Morsel would lose track of Shift; not used. Bit 6 of the `06` field byte shows the text at once. A 20-character text fits an encoder target (0x15). `9F 0B 7F` is echoed and makes feature CCs report. `B7 <cc> 00` (ch8) is answered with that feature's value as `B6 <cc> <value>`, e.g. `B7 54 00` → `B6 54 7F`. Once, right after the `B6 3F 00` test, `B7 03 00` came back as `B6 3F 7F`; after unplugging and replugging, every query answered as expected. | From [`firmware_notes.md`](firmware_notes.md#for-morsel-to-try-not-measured). Morsel doesn't use them yet. |

## Still to check by hand (Lab tab)
- Does temporary text (`showTemporary`) appear on top of the bitmap, and does the bitmap come back after the 1 s timeout?
- Bit order on the real screen: Lab → Screen bitmap → Border and Font. Check that the border touches all 4 edges and the text isn't mirrored. The firmware agrees with `Frame.pack`: rows top first, bit 6 = leftmost pixel, and x ≥ 128 is dropped ([firmware](firmware_notes.md#display)).
